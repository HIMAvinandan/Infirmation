Administrative Non-Disclosure

Public abstract of the Psychiatric Administration information-governance framework

Status: This page is an abstract. It is not intended to replace the full administrative instrument.

Purpose

The framework establishes protections for confidential, clinical, therapeutic, administrative, financial, technical, security, proprietary, research and evidentiary information handled within the Psychiatric Administration.

Core principles

  • Access according to lawful purpose, necessity and authority.
  • Minimum necessary involvement in disclosure and information handling.
  • Continuing confidentiality, subject to applicable law and lawful exceptions.
  • Protection of records, custody, integrity and provenance.
  • Controlled disclosure rather than unrestricted disclosure.
  • Security and incident-response procedures appropriate to the information involved.
  • Separation of financial administration from unnecessary clinical disclosure.
  • Technological neutrality, including future automated and AI-assisted processing.
  • Continuity through relocation, transfer, amendment and technological change.

Information lifecycle

Creation → Collection → Classification → Access → Use → Disclosure → Transmission → Storage → Modification → Archival → Transfer → Retention → Lawful Destruction.

Legal and professional qualification

The framework is expressly subject to mandatory applicable law, professional regulation, patient rights and competent judicial or regulatory authority. A published document should not be treated as creating an absolute confidentiality obligation where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted.
