Psychiatric Administration — Protection, Confidentiality, Non-Disclosure and Information Governance Protocol

I am introducing this Administration as the structured professional and administrative setting through which I undertake psychiatric, therapeutic, psychological, clinical, research, custodial and related authorised functions. The Aspirant is received within a protected therapeutic environment in which confidentiality, continuity, orderly administration and the integrity of records are treated as connected responsibilities rather than isolated formalities. This instrument therefore extends the practical commitments expressed in my earlier Confidentiality and Tariff documents: the professional relationship is to be approached deliberately; information is handled according to purpose, necessity and lawful authority; administrative and financial arrangements are kept distinct from unnecessary clinical disclosure; and the conditions of participation, transfer, communication and continuity are made explicit. The Administration is also designed to remain usable as circumstances, technologies, communications systems, records and administrative mechanisms change. Nothing in this introduction or in the instrument is intended to override mandatory law, professional regulation, patient rights or a competent lawful authority.
